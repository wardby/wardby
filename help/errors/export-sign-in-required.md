# export_sign_in_required

`export_sign_in_required` (HTTP 403). An anonymous share-link reader asked for a PNG or PDF. Sign in, or register as a guest on a comment link, and ask again; HTML exports need no sign-in.

See [Exporting designs and documents](../document-exports.md).
