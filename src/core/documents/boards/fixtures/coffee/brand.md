# Little Fern Coffee — Brand Guide

## Who we are

Little Fern Coffee is a neighbourhood coffee shop and bakery: a handful of
tables, a reading nook by the front window, and a weekend pastry menu that
people set alarms for. We are small on purpose. The name comes from the pot
of fern that has lived on the windowsill since the day the shop opened —
unhurried, easy to care for, quietly green all year.

## Tone of voice

Warm, plain-spoken, a little poetic without trying hard. We write like we
talk to a regular across the counter — short sentences, specific details
(the creak of the second stair, the smell of butter at 7am), no exclamation
marks stacked three deep, no "elevate your morning ritual" corporate-speak.

Do:
- "The kettle's always on, and so is the oven."
- "Pastries bake Saturday morning. They do not last until noon."
- "Pull up the green armchair. Stay as long as you like."

Don't:
- "Experience artisanal excellence."
- "Your premium coffee destination."
- Heavy use of jargon, hype, or urgency language.

## Colour palette

| Name            | Hex       | Use                                   |
|-----------------|-----------|----------------------------------------|
| Cream           | `#FBF3E7` | Page background                        |
| Paper White     | `#FFFDF9` | Cards, header, surfaces                |
| Espresso        | `#3B2A1D` | Body text                              |
| Fern            | `#3F5B45` | Primary accent, nav highlight          |
| Fern Dark       | `#2C4330` | Headings, footer background            |
| Terracotta      | `#C1623B` | Buttons, links, emphasis               |
| Gold            | `#D9A441` | Small accents, footer links, dividers  |
| Hairline        | `rgba(59,42,29,0.12)` | Borders, dividers         |

Backgrounds stay light and warm (cream/paper). Fern and terracotta carry the
contrast. Gold is used sparingly, like a glaze on top of a bun.

## Type

- **Headings:** a serif stack — `Georgia, "Iowan Old Style", "Palatino Linotype", serif`
  — set in Fern Dark, generous line spacing, nothing condensed.
- **Body & UI:** `system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", sans-serif`
  for menus, labels, and running text.
- Headings are set, not shouted: no all-caps body copy. Small labels (eyebrow
  text, nav tags) may use uppercase with slight letter-spacing.

## Shapes & motifs

- A circular badge containing a simple two-tone fern/leaf mark (fern green
  circle, gold and terracotta fronds, cream vein) is the logo. It appears on
  every board, linking to Home.
- Buttons are full pill shapes. Cards use soft 16px corners and a hairline
  border rather than heavy shadows.
- Dividers borrow the look of a handwritten chalkboard menu: dashed leader
  lines between an item name and its price.
- No stock photography. Illustration is done with CSS shapes, gradients,
  emoji, and small inline SVGs (a leaf, a cup, a lamp) — nothing literal or
  glossy.

## Consistency checklist for every board

- Same header: fern-badge logo (links to Home) + nav to Home / Menu / About / Visit.
- Same palette variables, same two type stacks.
- Pill buttons in Terracotta (primary) or outlined Fern (secondary).
- Footer in Fern Dark with Gold link accents and the shop's address/hours.
