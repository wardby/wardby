A neighbourhood coffee shop & bakery

Good coffee, a quiet corner, and pastries worth setting an alarm for.

Little Fern Coffee is small on purpose — a handful of tables, a reading nook by the window, and a kitchen that starts baking before the sun is up. The kettle's always on, and so is the oven.

Three good reasons to stay a while

☕

Just a few tables

No rows of laptops, no shouting over the grinder. Little Fern seats about twenty, so it stays the kind of place where the person behind the counter remembers your order.

📖

The reading nook

A green armchair, a lamp that's always a little too warm, and a shelf of paperbacks people leave behind on purpose. Take one. Leave one. No rush.

🥐

Weekend pastry menu

Saturday and Sunday mornings bring a short list of laminated pastries that sell out by eleven. Worth the early alarm.

This week in the nook

The shelf has a new stack of short story collections, the fern finally sprouted a new frond, and we're pouring a small-batch Ethiopian natural as our guest filter coffee — bright, a little like blackberries.

Drop by any morning for the regular menu, or save Saturday for the full pastry case.
